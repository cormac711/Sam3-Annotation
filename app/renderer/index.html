Step 2 of the pipeline: turn anonymous SAM 3 boxes into named characters. Load your image folder — the COCO annotations.json that 1_detect.py wrote into it is picked up automatically. Label every box, then EXPORT (COCO format) and feed the result to 3_export.py. The export is also your save file — load it back any time to resume.
